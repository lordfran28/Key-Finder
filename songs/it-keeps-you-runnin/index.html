It Keeps You Runnin’

For your voice and piano, in F major, on the same bars and timing as the guitar arrangement. 12 chords, each with one shape that stays the same every time it comes back. The left hand plays the root with the 7th or 5th; the right hand plays three notes close together.

The shapes in this song

Every right-hand shape, once, with the bass under it and the bars that use it.

How the hands work

Each chord has one shape for the whole song. When a chord comes back, your hands go back to the same keys.

Left hand: a shell, the root with the 7th (or the 5th) above it, low and held with the pedal.

Right hand: three notes close together. On 7th and 6th chords the root is left out, because the left hand has it.

The chords are the song’s own, played plainly. The guitar arrangement’s passing chords and colours are left out.

Practice order

  1. The 12 shapes on their own, one hand and then both, until you can find each without looking.
  2. Left hand alone with the track, then right hand alone, then together, slowly.
  3. Sing over it before the fills; add the fills last.
