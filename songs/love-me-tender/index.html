Love Me Tender

For your voice and piano, in D major, on the same bars and timing as the guitar arrangement. The left hand plays the root with the 7th or 5th; the right hand plays mostly three notes that move as little as possible from chord to chord.

The shapes in this song

Every right-hand shape, once, with the bass under it and the bars that use it.

How the hands work

Left hand: a shell, the root with the 7th (or the 5th) above it, low and held with the pedal. On a slash chord it plays only the note after the slash.

Right hand: mostly three notes close together. On 7th and 6th chords the root is left out, because the left hand has it. Each time, the right hand takes the shape nearest the one before, so most fingers stay where they are. Now and then a fourth finger adds one note for a fuller sound: where a part starts or ends, and on a long chord while you rest.

The chords are the song’s own, played plainly: no 9ths, 11ths or 13ths.

The fills

Only where you rest at the end of a bar, and no more than one in eight bars.

  • Bar R1: D major scale, 4 notes into D.
  • Bar C1.4: D major scale, 4 notes into B7.
  • Bar V2.4: D major scale, 4 notes into D6.
  • Bar V3.4: A Mixolydian scale, 4 notes into D6.
  • Bar End 2: G Dorian scale, 4 notes into D6.

Practice order

  1. Left hand alone with the track, then right hand alone, then together slowly.
  2. Sing over it before the fills; add the fills last.
