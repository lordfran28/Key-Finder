My One and Only Love

For your voice and piano, in C major, on the same bars and timing as the guitar arrangement. The left hand plays the root with the 7th or 5th; the right hand plays three notes that move as little as possible from chord to chord.

The shapes in this song

Every right-hand shape, once, with the bass under it and the bars that use it.

How the hands work

Left hand: a shell, the root with the 7th (or the 5th) above it, low and held with the pedal. On a slash chord it plays only the note after the slash.

Right hand: three notes close together, never four. On 7th and 6th chords the root is left out, because the left hand has it. Each time, the right hand takes the shape nearest the one before, so most fingers stay where they are.

The chords are the song’s own, played plainly: no 9ths, 11ths or 13ths.

The fills

Only where you rest at the end of a bar, and no more than one in eight bars.

  • Bar I2: G Mixolydian scale, 4 notes into Cmaj7.
  • Bar 14: D Mixolydian scale, 4 notes into Dm7.

Practice order

  1. Left hand alone with the track, then right hand alone, then together slowly.
  2. Sing over it before the fills; add the fills last.
